work. Nothing here is blocked on anything else.

## 1. The funded-account or activation number

**The highest-value figure missing from every document.** Plasma's JD is entirely about
"people who fund and keep spending". Every CV here proves she can buy installs cheaply at
volume. The gap between those two things is the main reason a hiring manager hesitates,
and one number closes it.

If any retention, activation or funded-rate figure sits behind the 3x install growth, it
turns "scaled volume" into "scaled valuable users". It does more for the Plasma
application than anything else on the page.

## 2. Job title and fixed term on the Plasma CV

The role header says "Growth Marketing Intern" and the profile opens with "Owns five
channels". A sceptical reader pairs those and assumes inflated scope. Two fixes, both
hers to choose:

- **Soften the verb.** "Runs paid across five channels and 26 markets" is just as strong
  and harder to argue with. The scope is genuinely unusual for an intern; let the figures
  make that point rather than the verb.
- **Show the fixed term.** The CV reads "May 2026 – Present" with no end date, so the
  fixed term has to be inferred from the word Intern. Either put the end date in
  ("May 2026 – Nov 2026") or add "6-month fixed term" to the sub-header. This is also what
  removes the worry about a 6-month tenure looking like a red flag: an internship ending is
  the normal thing, and a completed engagement with both dates reads as finished rather
  than abandoned.

The title must match whatever a reference check would confirm. A mismatch is the one thing
that kills an otherwise strong application outright.
