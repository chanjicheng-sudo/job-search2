- B2B payments: Airwallex Senior PMM EMEA; Checkout.com Manager PMM, Field Marketing Specialist (events/ABM) and Events Manager; Paddle Director of Demand Gen, Director of Self Serve and Organic & AI Search Manager (SEO); Payhawk Demand Gen Lead, Demand Gen Manager (needs a native European language), Senior Lifecycle and Senior Marketing Ops; Pleo Marketing Operations Manager.

## Pattern worth noting

- Trading apps and neobanks hire mostly at senior level right now. The level-matched roles
  are at Emma, Chase UK, Trading 212 and Zepz.
- B2B payments companies hire product marketing, demand gen and events, not paid app
  acquisition. Product marketing is the realistic way in, so the BVNK PMM CV is the base
  variant for that track.
- Apple Search Ads is named in Emma and Trading 212 postings. Lead with it.
- MMP (AppsFlyer/Adjust) is asked for at Trading 212, LemFi and Tide. Still the main gap.
