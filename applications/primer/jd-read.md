# Primer — Product Marketing Manager, Partners: hiring-manager read

Payments orchestration (one platform over many payment providers) for merchants' finance
and payments teams. $100m Series C. Fully remote. The role builds the partner marketing
engine with the Product Marketing Lead: positioning and messaging for partner features,
partner ecosystem campaigns, persona and journey work, adoption, engagement and
activation reporting, briefs and enablement material.

"Crypto" is used for Primer, at her request (Nov 2026): Primer is backed by Solana.

## Fit: good on launches, partners and writing, a stretch on B2B SaaS years

- **End-to-end launch:** the Malaysia loans launch she led (partners, Legal, compliance
  review, 4 HNW clients in 1 month).
- **Partner marketing:** KOL, affiliate and referral partners in Malaysia, 50+ assets
  supplied to 3 external acquisition partners, 30+ briefs.
- **Market insight to messaging:** perps research, retention +12%. Regional behaviour
  (Africa vs UK) used to tailor content.
- **Launch reporting:** automated dashboards, 128 structured tests.
- **B2B:** 20+ Institutional leads with the Senior B2B PMM, 3 major clients from MiCAR.
- **Writing across channels:** 24-language CRM localisation, the yield product relaunch.

  consumer-led company.

  merchant routes between processors (Adyen, Stripe, Checkout.com, Braintree), why

  fraud tools, alternative payment methods, apps in their ecosystem).

  (partners and merchants), so prepare one line on serving both sides, e.g. the Malaysia
  launch, where partners and clients both had to be won.
- **Challenge stage.** Expect to write messaging or a launch plan for a partner feature.
