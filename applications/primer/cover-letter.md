Dear Primer hiring team,

I am applying for the Product Marketing Manager role in your partner team.

I am a product marketer at Blockchain.com, working across Retail, Wealth and Institutional in 26 markets, with extensive experience in paid media. Running paid campaigns myself taught me what makes people act, so whether a product is new or already in market, I can always find the best way to scale it. I use AI to solve marketing problems, whether improving current workflows or opening new acquisition funnels.

On AI, our ads relied on real creators. Their videos were expensive, and once they ran out we had nothing new to test. I spotted competitors running AI-generated UGC ads and saw the downside of relying on creators, so I proposed we build our own. I built the workflow in 2 weeks. It cut production time by 75%, lifted click-through rate from 0.4% to 1.15%, and cut cost per install from $25 to $6. When we rebranded our yield product, I used it to recreate the creative under the new name, and it drove 600+ installs in the first 7 days. What I learnt is that a project that cannot be replicated at scale is not a good project. The first version relied on me to write the prompts, so I built a skill that generates them and handed the workflow to other team members to run. At Primer, I would use AI the same way, to produce partner campaigns and enablement material across channels without starting from a new template each time.

On launching a new product, I led the launch of our loans product in Malaysia on my own initiative. I assessed the paid opportunity, set the positioning, brand and tone, and ran campaigns with KOL, affiliate and referral partners, working with Legal on a new compliance review process. We converted 4 high net worth clients within the first month. Building a partner marketing engine is the same work, deciding the message, choosing the right partners and channels, and getting it to market.

On scaling a current product, I scaled our Apple Search Ads from 2 to 26 countries. Daily installs grew about 4x, from 200+ to 800+, while cost per install stayed flat. I ran 128 structured tests to get there, and on UK campaigns that cut cost per install by 21.5% while installs rose 30.2%. Paid media tells you within days which message lands and which does not. I would bring that habit to Primer, testing partner messaging early and reporting on adoption, engagement and activation so we always know what to scale.

On turning technical products into clear messaging, users were dropping off our perps product and I wanted to understand why. I led research with active, inactive and churned users and found that many did not understand how perps work or what the risks are. With Product, we made the product and risk explainer always visible and reworked the help page. Retention grew by 12%. Your partners and merchants are technical buyers, and I know that the message only works when they can see exactly what the feature does for them.

I want to join Primer because you are building the partner marketing engine from scratch, with no established process to lean on. I like that. I think you only really know why something is working, or why it is not, when you are involved in the whole process, from the positioning to the campaign to the report. I have built things from zero before, from our AI workflow to the Malaysia launch, and I would like to do it again in payments, where a better product reaches merchants through the partners they already trust.

I can share references from product marketing, compliance and legal colleagues at Blockchain.com.
