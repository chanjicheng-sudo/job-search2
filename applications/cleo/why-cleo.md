# Cleo — why Cleo and why me

Her angle (Nov 2026): AI focus, data leakage and hacks from general LLM tools, Cleo removes
that worry. Her voice: no contractions, colons, semicolons or em dashes.

---

**Why Cleo**

I like that Cleo is an AI built only for money. More and more people ask AI for help with their finances, but there have been a lot of data leaks and cyber hacks that come from LLM tools, and sharing your bank statements with a general chatbot means you do not know where that data ends up. With Cleo, users do not have to be concerned about that. It connects to your accounts securely and is built only for your money, so people can keep track of their finances easily and safely. I use AI every day to solve marketing problems, so I see both the value it brings and why people are careful with it.

**Why I am a good fit**

I care about growing efficiently, not just growing volume. At Blockchain.com, I scaled Apple Search Ads from 2 to 26 markets and ran 128 structured tests, scaling the 5 campaign groups that worked and cutting the 26 that did not. Daily installs grew about 4x, and UK cost per install fell by 21.5%. I also built our AI UGC workflow, which now runs on every paid platform including programmatic and cut cost per install from $25 to $6. I work with partners too, from the KOL, affiliate and referral partners behind our Malaysia launch to a $600,000 rewards campaign, where I learnt that a reward only works if it brings in the right users. This is how I would scale Cleo's programmatic and affiliate channels, keeping what brings in quality users and cutting what does not.
