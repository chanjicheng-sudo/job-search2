# Cleo — why Cleo and why me

Her angle (Nov 2026): AI focus, data leakage and hacks from general LLM tools, Cleo removes
that worry. Her voice: no contractions, colons, semicolons or em dashes.

---

**Why Cleo**

I like that Cleo is an AI built only for money. More and more people ask AI for help with their finances, but there have been a lot of data leaks and cyber hacks that come from LLM tools, and sharing your bank statements with a general chatbot means you do not know where that data ends up. With Cleo, users do not have to be concerned about that. It connects to your accounts securely and is built only for your money, so people can keep track of their finances easily and safely. I use AI every day to solve marketing problems, so I see both the value it brings and why people are careful with it.

**Why I am a good fit**

I take a growth approach to performance campaigns, identifying how users from different acquisition channels behave after they install. At Blockchain.com, we noticed that our users in Africa tend to take on more risk, while users in the UK are more balanced, so I do not treat every market the same. When I ran a $600,000 rewards campaign, prize hunters in Nigeria claimed the tokens and sold them straight away. The installs looked good, but they were not the users we wanted, so I moved the next wave to new users in the EU and the US. On our perps product, I surveyed active, inactive and churned users to understand why people left, and the changes we made with Product increased retention by 12%. This is how I would scale Cleo's channels, keeping what brings in quality users and cutting what does not.
