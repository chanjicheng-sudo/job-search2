# Propel Digital — Paid Social Consultant: hiring-manager read

Source: full JD sent by Fernando Faria (Rosslyn David) on 20 Nov 2026 after the
recruiter call. Recruiter-led, not applied directly.

## What the JD actually screens for

Strip the 30 bullets back and three things decide it:

1. **Creative performance, not just campaign management.** "Creative will be a central
   part of the role" and the list keeps coming back to it: concepts, briefing, AI
   production, Motion reporting, "why particular hooks and formats are performing". They
   want a media buyer who can also run the creative loop.
2. **Client-facing delivery.** Weekly and monthly client calls, presenting performance,
   advising on content, managing deliverables "independently". This is an agency job.
3. **Meta first.** "Strong platform expertise in Meta Ads, alongside working knowledge of
   the other core platforms." The five-platform list is the agency's footprint, not a bar
   every candidate clears.

The process confirms it: stage 3 is a one-hour strategy audit presented to leadership.
That stage, not the CV, is where the hire is decided.

## Where she is strong

- **AI creative production.** The exact thing they describe: "use AI creative tools to
  develop and refine static, video and other paid social assets". She built the AI UGC
  workflow from zero, evaluated 6 model providers, cut production 75% (~2 hours to 30
  minutes), and 100+ videos ran as paid social. Strongest single hook. Most candidates at
  1–2 years will have used AI tools, not built a production line.
- **Creative testing with numbers attached.** Paid social CTR 0.4% to 1.15%, paid social
  CPI $25 to $6. Label both as paid social (see the performance note).
- **Creative strategy and briefing.** 30+ briefs, 40 ads across 9 channels, creative
  delivery directed to 3 external partners with 50+ assets.
- **Structured testing.** 128 tests on Apple Search Ads plus an MRes in experimental
  design. Answers "structured creative testing plans" directly.
- **Budget ownership.** £50k+ monthly at Malaberg at 120% ROAS. Answers "manage campaign
  budgets, pacing and forecasts".
- **Reporting and automation.** Automated reporting at both jobs. Answers "Propel's
  technology and automation tools" and the line about the Solutions team.
- **Platforms covered:** Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn all appear in the record.

## Fit

Strong on the creative half, which is the half they say is central. Credible on the
media-buying half. Weak on agency experience.

It is also a **step down in level and pay** compared with the other live processes.
"Consultant" is the bottom of their ladder (Consultant, Senior Consultant, Associate
Director, Director). The JD contradicts itself on pay: £35–45k at the top, £35–40k in
the offer section. Fernando quoted £35–45k "or a touch more". Carmoola is £50–60k. Worth
settling the number with Fernando before investing in the strategy task.

## Gaps that would get probed

- **No agency or client-facing experience.** Both jobs were in-house. The JD says
  "ideally within an agency or consultancy". Closest evidence: directing 3 external
  partners, and weekly Compliance reviews (presenting work to a stakeholder who can say
  no). She needs one story of presenting performance to someone outside her team.
- **Reddit and YouTube.** No evidence of either in the record. The AI UGC ran on Google
  Ads, which may have included YouTube placements. Confirm before claiming it.
- **Motion.** Not used. They accept "a comparable creative analytics platform". Her
  answer is that she built her own reporting. Better: try Motion's free trial before the
  discovery call so she can speak to it.
- **Meta depth.** Meta is listed in her skills but the headline numbers are Apple Search
  Ads and TikTok. Expect a hard Meta question (campaign structure, Advantage+, CAPI). Have
  one Meta campaign she can walk through end to end.
- **Tracking and troubleshooting.** They want tracking and measurement understanding.
  She set up ad accounts and conversion tracking, which is fair to claim. Do not drift
  into MMP or Meta AEM territory (see "Not claimed" in the performance note).
