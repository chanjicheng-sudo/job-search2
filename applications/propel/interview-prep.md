# Propel Digital — Paid Social Consultant: interview prep

Performance marketing consultancy, London (Farringdon office, 1 day in, 4 from home).
Founded 2018 by Rob and Jack (ex-Periscopix and Brainlabs). Salary £35–45k (body of the
spec also says £35–40k). Agency, not fintech/mobile app (outside her usual target).

## Process
1. Recruiter call and CV review
2. **Paid social discovery call** (30 min, paid social team): experience, history, hands-on campaign work
3. **Strategy task** (1 hour, paid social + leadership): audit and present a plan for an example client
4. Final in person (30 min) with paid social leadership and one or both co-founders

## The role in one line
Run clients' paid social (Meta first, plus LinkedIn, TikTok, Reddit, YouTube) end to end,
own creative testing with AI tools and Motion, and present results on weekly and monthly
client calls.

## Gaps (do not apologise)
- **Reddit and YouTube ads:** not on record. YouTube → your UGC runs on Google (the Italy
  ad is a YouTube link). Reddit → same principles, native-looking creative, community tone.
- **Motion:** not used. Nearest → you analyse creative by hook, message and format in
  platform data and your own dashboards. Learn Motion's basics (creative reports by
  hook, format, asset, thumb-stop and hold rates).
- **Agency and client-facing:** your "clients" were internal teams and 3 external
  partners. Frame it as presenting to the CMO, Product, Legal and partners.

## Likely discovery call questions
- **Walk me through your paid social experience.** Malaberg (Meta, ecommerce, £50k+/month,
  120% ROAS) → Blockchain.com (Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Google, AI UGC across 26 markets).
- **How do you structure a Meta account?** Few campaigns, broad audiences with Advantage+,
  separate prospecting and retargeting, a creative testing campaign feeding winners into
  scaling, budget on what converts.
- **How do you run a creative test?** One variable at a time (hook, message, format), enough
  spend to read it, judge on CPA or ROAS not CTR alone, scale winners, retire fatigued ads.
- **Tell me about an ad that worked and why.** The Italy UGC ad.
- **How do you handle a client when results drop?** Diagnose (tracking, fatigue, auction,
  landing page), tell them early, bring a fix and a next step.
- **Why agency / why Propel?** Variety of clients and industries, many problems to solve,
  one size does not fit all. Bespoke model, not a traditional agency.

## Strategy task framework (for stage 3)
1. **Objective and KPIs:** business goal → primary KPI (CPA, ROAS, lead quality) and guardrails.
2. **Structure:** objectives, audiences (broad, lookalike, retargeting), placements per platform.
3. **Testing:** creative testing plan (hooks, formats, messages), budget split for tests.
4. **Scaling:** move winners into scale campaigns, raise budget 20–30% at a time.
5. **Creative strategy:** UGC, statics, video, AI to iterate fast, Motion to read results.
6. **Reporting:** weekly call (pacing, top creatives, actions), monthly (trends, strategy).
7. **Problems:** tracking checks, fatigue, rising CPA, a clear priority order.

## Questions to ask
1. How are client teams set up, and how many clients would I look after?
2. How do the consultants and the creative team split the creative work?
3. What does success look like in the first 6 months?

## Watch-outs
- "Graduate Growth Marketing Manager", never "intern". Don't claim Reddit, YouTube ads or Motion.
- Salary: their band is below your £45–50k. Decide your number before the call.
