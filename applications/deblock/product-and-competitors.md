# Deblock — product and competitors

Researched 12 Nov 2026 for the Junior Performance Marketing Manager interview (Italy and
France paid acquisition). Companion to `interview-prep.md`.

**How far to trust this.** Revolut's pricing page and deblock.com could not be fetched.
Several figures come from review sites, and the main Deblock pricing source is a review
**sponsored by Deblock**. Anything marked *check* should be confirmed in the app before
you quote it. Never quote a rate or fee in the interview that you have not seen yourself.

> **Update 13 Nov 2026 — the 4% yield.** An October 2026 source says the full 4% is
> **Premium only, with 2% on Standard**. Read "4%" below as "up to 4% on Premium".
> Deblock presents the yield as **self-custodial**: the euros become EURCV in the
> user's own wallet and are deposited into a Morpho vault run by Steakhouse and
> Gauntlet. Deblock says it cannot block or move them. The flip side is that they sit
> **outside e-money safeguarding**, and a vault failure could lose them. Deblock tops up
> the rate from a reserve when the vault pays less than 4%. After France's 31.4% flat
> tax, 4% is about 2.74% net. Check the current rates in the app.

## The product, in plain terms

| | What it is | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Euro current account | French IBAN. Visa debit card: physical, virtual and single-use |
| Crypto | **Self-custodial** wallet in the same app | The customer holds the keys, not Deblock. 150+ cryptos |
| Yield | "Up to 4%" variable on the euro balance | Converted to EURCV (Société Générale-Forge euro stablecoin) and placed in the Morpho DeFi protocol. **Not a bank deposit. No €100k guarantee.** Some French reviews say the Standard plan earns less than 4%. *Check* |
| Metals | Physical gold, silver, platinum, palladium from €1 | Swiss vaults. Fees up to 0.49%. No custody fee |
| Round-ups | Card spend rounded up into Bitcoin (May 2026) or gold (Nov 2026) | |
| Insurance | Free purchase insurance on the card | Stays active with one card payment a month. Premium adds travel and rental-car cover |
| Business | Business accounts | Waitlist, planned Q4 2026 |

**Possible fee change, unverified:** one French snippet said Standard starts charging
0.5–1% on non-euro card payments from 11 Nov 2026. *Check in the app.*

**Promotions** (third-party sources, *check*):
- Referral link: a random bonus up to €500 (usually €20) plus 3 months of Premium, after
  a €25 deposit.
- GTA VI: code PAYDAY, €1,000 card spend within 90 days, game refunded up to €79.99.
  Runs 20 Oct to 31 Dec 2026.

**Licences:** e-money institution (ACPR, Banque de France). MiCA crypto licence (AMF).
It is **not a bank**. Revolut, Trade Republic and N26 are.

## How it differs: one line

> The only app here that gives you a euro current account and a crypto wallet **you own
> the keys to**, in one place, under a French MiCA licence.

Everyone else is one or the other:
- **Neobanks and brokers** (Revolut, Trade Republic, N26) have a full account but hold
  your crypto for you.
- **Exchanges** (Bitpanda, Crypto.com, Coinbase) hold your crypto and are not a current
  account.
- **Self-custody cards** (Gnosis Pay, MetaMask Card, Bleap) let you keep your keys but
  have no proper euro account with an IBAN.

Physical metals from €1 and gold round-ups add a second difference that has nothing to do
with crypto. That matters in Italy, where gold is a familiar asset.

## Where Deblock wins and where it does not

**Wins**
- Self-custody plus a real euro account. Nobody else in this list has both.
- French MiCA licence. EU-native and regulated.
- Metals from €1, gold and Bitcoin round-ups. Easy, low-commitment first actions after
  sign-up, which helps activation.
- Free card insurance that requires one payment a month. A built-in retention habit.

**Matched or weaker**
- **Trust and scale.** ~20k Italian users against Revolut's ~6M. Revolut and Trade
  Republic are banks. Deblock is not.
- **The yield.** "4%" looks better than Trade Republic's 2.5–3% or Revolut's tiered rates.
  But theirs are guaranteed up to €100k and Deblock's is not. A careful saver notices.
  This is the main objection, and the riskiest ad claim.
- **Fees.** 1.99% on Standard is higher than Bitpanda, Paymium and Trade Republic. Premium
  at 0.49% is competitive but costs €120–180 a year.
- **Asset range.** 150+ is fewer than Revolut (280+) and Bitpanda (600+).

## Crypto cards and stablecoin neobanks

Added after a second research pass. Many of these figures come from competitor blogs
(Bleap writes about its rivals) and affiliate review sites. The most neutral overview is
[DeFiprime, Sep 2026](https://defiprime.com/crypto-debit-cards-2026).

### Five models in the market

1. **A card attached to a self-custody wallet.** The card spends straight from your
   wallet. Examples: MetaMask Card, Bleap, Gnosis Pay, Plasma One. Main risk: smart
   contracts and the card programme manager. Both failed this year (see below).
2. **Borrow to spend.** You spend a loan against your ETH or BTC instead of selling it,
   so there is no taxable sale. ether.fi Cash. Risk: liquidation and interest.
3. **The operator holds your money,** often through an offshore company. KAST, RedotPay.
4. **Exchange or broker card.** Your crypto is sold when you pay. Revolut, Bitpanda,
   Crypto.com, OKX.
5. **Regulated euro account plus wallet.** A real euro IBAN you can be paid into, under
   an EU licence, with crypto and on-chain yield alongside. **This is Deblock.** Holyheld
   is the nearest copy, with a weaker licence (Lithuanian VASP, MiCA pending).

  2026, issued by Monavate under MiCA.

### Where Deblock differs from crypto cards

- **It is a current account first.** A French IBAN for salary and direct debits.
  Most crypto cards have no IBAN of your own.
- **Euros, not dollars.** The yield is on a euro stablecoin from Société Générale-Forge.
  No currency risk and no reward token. Plasma One is USDT. Most cashback is paid in
  tokens (XPL, mUSD) whose value moves.
- **Regulated in France.** EMI plus the first AMF MiCA licence. Several card rivals have
  no EU licence of their own and rely on a card issuer.
- **Caveat: Deblock also uses DeFi.** The yield runs through Morpho, and one source says
  the DeFi access sits outside the MiCA licence, through a separate company (Techblock
  Ltd). So **do not claim "no smart-contract risk"**. Yield figures quoted in 2026 range
  from 4% to "up to 6%". Check the current rate in the app.

### Where crypto cards beat Deblock

- Cashback of 1–4%. Deblock does not compete on it.
- Higher headline yield (Plasma One up to 5%, in dollars).
- Borrowing against crypto without selling it (ether.fi).
- Global reach. KAST and Plasma One target 150+ countries.
- Many are free with no FX fee. Deblock's lower crypto fee needs Premium at €14.99.

### For paid acquisition

- **Crypto-native audience** (X, YouTube, Telegram): ether.fi, Bleap, MetaMask and
  Plasma One fight here with referral codes and affiliate review sites. Bleap publishes
  Italian and French comparison pages. Do not get pulled into a cashback war against
  token-funded rewards.
- **What Deblock can own:** "a real euro IBAN, not a top-up card", "licensed in France",
  "yield on euros, not dollars".
- **Live opportunity: Gnosis Pay users.** Gnosis Pay's customers are EU users who keep
  their own keys and spend euros. That is Deblock's audience exactly, and their card stops
  on 20 Dec 2026. A "moving from Gnosis Pay?" search and social campaign has a five-week
  window. **This is a good idea to bring to the interview.** Check that Deblock has not
  already done it, and frame it as a test.

## What this means for paid acquisition (her angle in the interview)

**Who she would be bidding against**
- **Italy:** Revolut has the biggest brand and search presence. Trade Republic is
  spending heavily: its biggest-ever campaign, with Brad Pitt (May 2026), plus a 3%
  headline rate. Young Platform and Conio/Hype compete on "crypto" and "bitcoin" terms.
- **France:** Revolut, Trade Republic and BoursoBank on "néobanque" and "compte
  rémunéré". Paymium, Coinhouse and the exchanges on "acheter bitcoin".
- That Revolut and Trade Republic outspend Deblock is an inference, not data. Look them
  up in the **Meta Ad Library** and the **Google Ads Transparency Center** before the
  interview. Twenty minutes there gives her something nobody else will bring.

**The regulatory point (strong in an interview)**
In 2026 Italy's competition authority (**AGCM**) fined **Revolut €11.5M** and **Trade
Republic €2.5M** over advertising and cost disclosure. Any "fino al 4%" ad in Italy needs
clear risk wording. Her regulated-copy work is relevant here. Say it as "I would agree the
yield claim with Compliance before testing it". Do not lecture them about their own
product.

**Angles worth testing, as hypotheses**
- Against **Revolut and Trade Republic**: "your crypto, your keys". The one thing they
  cannot say.
- Against **exchanges**: a full euro account and card, not just a trading app.
- In **Italy**: physical gold from €1. Familiar, no crypto knowledge needed, and none of
  the deposit-guarantee questions the 4% raises.
- **Activation**: round-ups and the monthly-payment insurance give new users a reason to
  spend on the card, which is the action the GTA promo pays for.
