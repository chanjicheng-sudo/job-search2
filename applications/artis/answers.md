# Artis — application answers

## Why do you want to work at Artis?

Her angle (Nov 2026): commodity trading is about time, Artis organises the workflow, and
its API makes the data useful for AI. No colons, her voice.

> Trading commodities is all about time, especially for arbitrage and prop traders, where a few seconds can decide whether a trade is worth taking. Artis gives traders and brokers an organised workflow, with live prices, shared curves and one view of the market, so they can act faster and with more confidence. That is a real pain point, and I think Artis solves it well. I also like that Artis offers its data through an API. As people use AI more, having a live database you can connect to AI tools is extremely useful. I see this in my own work, where I connect our data to Claude through MCP connectors and APIs to automate our reporting. I would love to help more traders discover what Artis can do.

## Background on Artis (for the interview)

- Software for commodity traders and brokers (oil, gas, freight, emissions, agriculture
  and more): live exchange and broker data, shared price curves, collaboration, snapshots,
  Excel add-in, REST and streaming API, mobile.
- Product values: Provenance, Granularity, Versatility, One Global Heartbeat.
- Founder and CEO Sebastian Ferraccù (about 18 years trading oil). Offices in London,
  Houston, Dubai, Singapore. Users shown on their site include Glencore, Trafigura,
  ExxonMobil, ADNOC, Macquarie.
- Funded competitor in the space: Sparta ($42m Series B).
- Round 2 is a growth exercise: how to reach a group of customers and generate trials.
  Structure: segment, message, channel, test, measure.

Sources: artis.works, artis.works/how-it-works, artis.works/for/enterprise, CB Insights.
