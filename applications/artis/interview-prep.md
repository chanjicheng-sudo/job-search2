## Commodity trading basics (so you sound fluent)

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| Physical vs paper trading | Buying and moving real barrels or cargoes vs trading futures and swaps on price |
| Trader | Buys and sells for a trading house, oil major or bank |
| Broker | Matches buyers and sellers and earns commission. Sends prices to traders all day |
| Forward curve | Expected price for each future month (e.g. Brent for Jan, Feb, Mar…) |
| Arbitrage | Profiting from a price gap between two places, times or products |
| Prop trader | Trades the firm's own money |
| Marking the book | End-of-day valuing of open positions at current prices (Artis snapshots) |
| ICE, CME | The main commodity exchanges |

## The growth exercise (round 2)

They will ask how you would reach a specific group and generate trials. Use this shape:

1. **Who exactly.** Pick one segment and say why (size, pain, reachable).
2. **What they care about.** Speed, trust in prices (provenance), working across time
   zones and on mobile.
3. **The message.** One line per segment, e.g. for brokers, "Stream your curves to every
   client, live and secure, on any device".
4. **Channels and sequence.** Research list (ZoomInfo) → LinkedIn and email outreach →
   content (a short market-theme post or webinar) → event follow-up. Ask existing users
   for referrals, because of the network effect.
5. **The offer.** A free trial (Artis Lite has a 30-day OB Curves trial).
6. **Measure and iterate.** Trial sign-ups, activation, conversion to paid, cost per
   trial, by segment and message. Keep what works, cut the rest (your 128 tests story).
