# Atominvest — Graduate Technical Product Analyst: hiring-manager read

SaaS operating system for private markets asset managers (PE, growth equity, private
credit). The role supports the API, SSO, MCP and SFTP capabilities and the Outlook, Excel
and Word plugins with the Technical Product Manager and Engineering: discovery,
requirements, first-line triage with Postman and logs, AI-assisted defect investigation,
testing, R&D proofs of concept, documentation. Claude and Claude Code are named tools.
£37k to £40k.

Not crypto, so "digital assets" in everything for Atominvest.

## Blocker to check first

"Unrestricted and permanent right to work in the UK", no sponsorship now or in future.
A dependant visa gives the right to work but is time-limited and tied to the partner's
visa, so it is not permanent. Ask the recruiter before investing in a cover letter.
