# Crypto Banter — Growth Marketing Manager, Banter Bubbles: hiring-manager read

Banter Bubbles is Crypto Banter's free market app (10,000+ coins and stocks as live
bubbles, news, watchlists, portfolio tracker). The job: turn app users into verified
signups on the partner exchange. Owns the funnel (traffic, activation, registration, KYC),
experiments, in-product conversion moments, live show and creator traffic, tracking,
affiliate and referral flows, weekly reporting. Remote.

Crypto-native, so "crypto" throughout.
