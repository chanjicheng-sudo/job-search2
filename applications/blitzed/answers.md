**1. What's one marketing campaign you personally owned that delivered strong results?**

At Blockchain.com, our ads relied on real creators, who were expensive and ran out fast. A TikTok ad usually lasts about two weeks, so we needed new creative all the time. I noticed competitors running AI-generated UGC ads, so I proposed building our own and built the workflow in 2 weeks. I wrote every script myself so the ads sounded like real people, not generic AI. It cut production time by 75%, lifted click-through rate from 0.4% to 1.15% (+188%) and cut cost per install from $25 to $6 (−76%). When we relaunched our yield product, I used it to recreate the creative, and it drove 600+ installs in the first 7 days. It now runs on every paid platform, and I built a skill that writes the prompts so the team can run it without me.
