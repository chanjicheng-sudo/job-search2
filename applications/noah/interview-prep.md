# Noah — Digital Marketing interview prep

First dedicated marketing hire. Everything below is built from the performance note and
public coverage (sources at the end). Answers are in her voice: short, plain, numbers.

---

## 1. Noah in 60 seconds

- **What they do.** An API that lets businesses move money with stablecoins. Fiat in,
  stablecoin across, local payout out. Virtual accounts, conversion and payouts on local
  rails. Partners include Circle and Paxos.
- **Coverage.** 150+ countries and 60+ currencies (October 2026 release). Earlier coverage
  said 70 countries, so use "150+ countries" as the current figure.
- **People.** Shah Ramezani, co-founder and CEO. Thijn Lamers, co-founder and president,
  was on the founding team at Adyen.
- **Money.** $22m seed led by LocalGlobe (June 2025), extended by $16m in October 2026 to
  $38m (Endeit Capital, FJ Labs, LocalGlobe, Felix Capital).
- **Momentum.** Revenue up 538% year to date in 2026. 150+ new customers this year across
  remittances, fintech, marketplaces and payroll. Building to support 4 to 5x current
  revenue in the next 12 months.
- **Next.** US expansion and a New York office.
- **Partnerships.** NALA (January 2026, instant settlement for emerging-market payments),
  Gnosis (USD virtual accounts), Ledger (embedded in its wallet).
- **Licences.** One source says a MiCA licence and a payment institution licence from
  Latvijas Banka in September 2026. Single source. Check noah.com before mentioning it.

**Why they are hiring now.** They just raised, they are entering the US, and they need
4 to 5x revenue. Today growth comes from founders and sales. They need someone who can
build pipeline that does not depend on the founders' network.

## 3. Likely questions and the story to use

**"Tell us about a campaign you scaled."** Apple Search Ads, 2 to 26 countries, 9 to 121
campaigns, daily installs about 4x (200+ to 800+) at flat cost per install. 128 structured
tests, scaled the 5 groups that worked, cut 26 that did not. UK cost per install −21.5%
while installs rose 30.2%.

**"Have you run X ads? What worked?"** Yes. **Bring your own numbers**: markets, spend,
best ad, cost per result. They wrote "with the results to prove it", so one concrete
result matters more than a list of channels.

**"How good is your SEO?"** Be precise. You do search optimisation on App Store and
Google Play, refreshing listings across 20 storefronts and building 20+ Custom Product
Pages for 12 markets. Then bridge to what you would do for Noah.

> The principles are the same. Find what people search for, match the page to their
> intent, and test. For Noah I would start with the payout and on-ramp searches where
> buyers compare providers, and I would also make sure Noah is the answer when someone
> asks ChatGPT or Claude which stablecoin payments API to use.

**"How would you generate B2B pipeline?"** The 100-operator database (20+ active leads)
and the MiCAR story (spotted the change, built a list of 80 publishers with AI, 3 major
clients). Use the line you approved:

> I like that marketing generates the leads for sales. One-to-one outreach is hard to make
> efficient, while marketing can reach a wide audience and pass on the people who are ready.

**"Tell us about a time you improved conversion."** Perps research with active, inactive
and churned users, found they did not understand the product, explainer and help page
reworked, retention +12%. Malaberg landing pages, daily conversions 10 to 50+.

**"How do you use AI?"** AI UGC workflow, built in 2 weeks. Production −75%, CTR 0.4% to
1.15%, CPI $25 to $6, 600+ installs in the first 7 days after you recreated the creative
for the rebranded yield product. The lesson: a project that cannot be replicated at scale
is not a good project, so you built a skill and handed it to the team. Then the reporting
dashboard with Claude Code and MCP connectors, about 10 hours a month saved.

**"How do you measure marketing?"** Their JD says "build the dashboard before you launch
the campaign". That is what you did. Automated reporting first, then the tests, so every
decision had data behind it.

## 5. First 90 days (have this ready if asked)

1. **Weeks 1 to 4. Measure and listen.** Set up tracking and a pipeline dashboard. Talk to
   sales and 10 customers across remittances, payroll, marketplaces and fintech. Find
   which segment closes fastest.
2. **Weeks 4 to 8. Build the base.** Website pages per segment and per corridor. Case
   studies from the 150+ customers signed this year. An account list for the US launch,
   the same way you built the 100-operator database.
3. **Weeks 8 to 12. Test and scale.** Paid search on high-intent terms, LinkedIn and X to
   the account list, founder content each week. Keep what brings pipeline, cut the rest.

## 7. Noah vs its competitors

All of these use stablecoins to move money across borders for businesses. They differ in
**who owns them, who they sell to, and what they lead with.**

| | Owner | Sells to | Leads with | Scale signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Noah** | Independent startup (Adyen founding-team DNA) | Fintechs, marketplaces, payroll, remittance firms | Payouts on local rails in 150+ countries, incl. bank, wallets, mobile money and cash, with a rules engine | 538% revenue growth in 2026, 150+ new customers |
| **Bridge** | Stripe (bought for $1.1bn, Oct 2024) | Developers and platforms, many already on Stripe | Stablecoin accounts (~100 countries), issuing your own stablecoin, Visa stablecoin cards | Zepz/Sendwave, Phantom, Chipper Cash |
| **BVNK** | Reported Mastercard acquisition, ~$1.8bn (Mar 2026, unconfirmed) | Large payment companies and enterprises | Stablecoin wallets and settlement inside existing payment flows, Visa Direct pilot | $30bn+ a year, Deel, Rapyd, Corpay, LemFi |
| **Ripple (+ Rail)** | Ripple, bought Rail for $200m (2025) | Banks and large financial institutions | Its own stablecoin (RLUSD) and token (XRP), treasury | Rail claimed 10%+ of B2B stablecoin payments |
| **Conduit** | Independent, $36m Series A (2025) | Importers and exporters, B2B trade | Cross-border B2B payments in Latin America, Africa and Asia | $10bn+ a year, fewer countries |

**How Noah is different, in plain words**

1. **It is independent.** Bridge belongs to Stripe, BVNK reportedly to Mastercard, and
   Ripple has its own coin to push. Noah is not tied to a card network, a payments giant
   or a token. A platform that does not want to depend on Stripe or Mastercard has a
   reason to pick Noah.
2. **It is built around the last mile.** Competitors lead with accounts, wallets or
   issuing a stablecoin. Noah leads with getting money out in local currency, including
   mobile money and cash, which matters most in emerging markets (see the NALA deal).
3. **It sells to fast-moving mid-size businesses.** Ripple targets banks and BVNK targets
   large payment companies. Noah's 150+ new customers this year are fintechs, payroll
   and marketplace companies that want one API and to go live quickly.
4. **Its weakness is awareness.** It is the smallest name in this table. That is the
   problem this role exists to fix.

**What to say if they ask how you would position Noah**

> The two biggest names now belong to Stripe and Mastercard, and Ripple has its own coin
> to promote. I think Noah can own being the independent option, with the widest payout
> coverage. I would build comparison pages for searches like "Bridge alternatives", and
> make sure Noah comes up when someone asks an AI tool which stablecoin payout API to use.

**Careful**
- BVNK: you applied there. Do not mention it. The Mastercard deal is reported by trackers,
  not confirmed in a primary source here, so say "reportedly" if it comes up.
- Ask them who they lose deals to most. Their answer beats this table.
