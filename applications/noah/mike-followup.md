**1. My mix of work**

Most of my work is consumer acquisition and UGC, run hands-on, alongside institutional lead generation, where I built prospect lists and outreach for our business development team.

The case that shows both what I run myself and what I lead through others is our AI UGC workflow. Our paid social relied on real creators, which was expensive, and on TikTok an ad only lasts about two weeks, so we kept running out of fresh creative. I saw competitors running AI-generated UGC and proposed we build our own. I tested AI models for avatar, voice, motion and video, and spent two weeks on prompt engineering until the output was good enough to run as ads. Before launch, I took the first assets to Legal with competitor examples so we agreed what was acceptable for a regulated product. The first version depended on me writing every prompt, so I built a skill that generates them and handed the workflow to the team, who now run it without me. The results are on my CV.

**2. Ramping up fast**

When I took on our loans launch in Malaysia, there was no playbook, no local team and no compliance process for the market. In the first two weeks I researched the local regulations and competitors and assessed which channels were viable. I then worked with Legal on tone and risk disclosures and helped set up a compliance review for the new market. I chose KOL, affiliate and referral partners, because for high net worth clients there, trust and referrals carry more weight than ads. We converted 4 high net worth clients in the first month.

**3. My work against what I described**

When I joined, Apple Search Ads ran in 2 countries with 9 campaigns. I expanded market by market and built a Custom Product Page for each one, so the page matched what people searched for. Every expansion ran as a structured test, 128 in total, and I moved budget from the campaigns that underperformed into the groups that scaled. Daily installs grew about 4x while cost per install stayed flat. [LINK to a public Custom Product Page]

**4. A landing page I built end to end**

My standout is the landing page rebuild at Malaberg. I used funnel analysis and session recordings in Microsoft Clarity to see exactly where people dropped off, then redesigned the pages in Figma and Photoshop to remove that friction. I tracked conversions daily against the old pages until the uplift held, which is how I knew it worked. Daily conversions grew 5x. [LINK or before-and-after screenshots]
