Below is the story behind the work on my CV, with a few public examples linked. Much of my work sits in internal ad accounts and dashboards that I cannot share outside Blockchain.com, so I have described those in words. I am very happy to talk any of it through on a call.

**1. My mix of work**

*Consumer and UGC (most of my work)*

- **AI UGC, built hands-on.** Our paid social relied on real creators, which was expensive, and on TikTok an ad only lasts about two weeks, so we kept running out of fresh creative. I saw competitors running AI-generated UGC and proposed we build our own. I tested AI models for avatar, voice, motion and video, then spent two weeks on prompt engineering until the output was good enough to run as ads. Before launch, I took the first assets to Legal with competitor examples so we agreed what was acceptable for a regulated product. The first version depended on me writing every prompt, so I built a skill that generates them and handed the workflow to the team, who now run it without me. When we rebranded our yield product, every creator video still carried the old name, and we used the workflow to have fresh creative live from day one.
- **Apple Search Ads, run hands-on.** When I joined, search ran in 2 countries with 9 campaigns. I expanded market by market, set bids per country and built a Custom Product Page for each market so the page matched the search. Every expansion ran as a structured test, 128 in total, and I moved budget from the campaigns that underperformed into the groups that scaled. Daily installs grew about 4x while cost per install stayed flat. I built automated reporting with Claude Code so I could see cost per install by market every morning, which made those decisions fast. The data also showed users in Africa took more risk than users in the UK, so we tailored creative by region.
- **Retention research, designed and run by me.** Users were leaving our perps product. I ran three rounds of research across active, inactive and churned users and found that many did not understand how perps work or the risks. I worked with Product to make the explainer always visible and rework the help page.
- **Led through others.** I wrote the creative briefs for our designers and three external acquisition partners, worked with Data Engineering and Security to turn my reporting into a company-wide dashboard, and worked with Front-End Engineering on refreshed product pages.

*Institutional*

- I researched and built a prospect database of 100 operators for our Institutional business development team and handed qualified leads to them to convert.
- When MiCA came in, I noticed a major exchange was losing access to its European clients. I saw a window, built an outreach list of 80 crypto publishers with AI and ran the outreach myself. It brought in 3 major high net worth clients.

**2. Ramping up fast**

When I took on our loans launch in Malaysia, there was no playbook, no local team and no compliance process for the market. In the first two weeks I researched the local regulations and competitors and assessed which channels were viable. I then worked with Legal on tone and risk disclosures and helped set up a compliance review for the new market. I chose KOL, affiliate and referral partners, because for high net worth clients there, trust and referrals carry more weight than ads. We converted 4 high net worth clients in the first month.
