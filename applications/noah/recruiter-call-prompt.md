Copy everything below the line into a separate LLM chat.

---

You are my interview coach. Tomorrow I have an intro call with a recruiter at Noah for
their Digital Marketing role, the company's first dedicated marketing hire. Help me prepare
by running a realistic mock recruiter screen, then giving me feedback.

## How to run the session

1. Start by asking which stage this is (in-house talent partner or external agency) and
   how long the call is. If I do not know, assume 30 minutes with an in-house recruiter.
2. Run a mock call. Ask one question at a time, as the recruiter would, and wait for my
   answer. Mix the standard screening questions (section 6) with role-specific ones.
3. After each answer, give short feedback. Say what landed, what was vague or too long,
   and give a tighter version in my voice. Keep answers to about 60 to 90 seconds spoken.
4. Push back like a real recruiter when an answer is vague, and probe my gaps (section 5).
5. At the end, give me a one-page cheat sheet: my 60-second introduction, three stories
   with their numbers, my answers to the logistics questions, and 3 questions to ask.

**My voice:** short, plain, declarative sentences. No jargon, no buzzwords, no
"passionate", no "leverage". Numbers over adjectives. Do not invent facts or numbers. If
you need a fact about me that is not below, ask me.

## 6. Recruiter screening questions to cover

- Walk me through your background. (Keep to 60 seconds. Lead with the move into fintech.)
- Why are you leaving Blockchain.com? (Fixed-term internship ending. Do not criticise it.)
- Why Noah, and why this role?
- What do you know about Noah and what we do?
- Why did you leave a full-time job for an internship?
- What are your salary expectations? (Help me research a range for a first marketing hire
  at a seed-stage London fintech before I answer, and practise giving a range, not a
  single number.)

- Right to work and sponsorship. (Dependant visa, right to work, no sponsorship needed.)
- Are you interviewing elsewhere? (Honest, without naming companies. Do not mention BVNK,
  a competitor I applied to.)
- Location and hybrid or remote preferences.
- What would you do in your first 90 days?
- A campaign you are proud of, with the numbers.

- What does the interview process look like from here, and who will I meet?

- What would success look like after 6 months?
- What made you decide to hire the first marketer now?
