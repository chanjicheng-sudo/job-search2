# Noah — company background for the in-person interview

Researched 18 Nov 2026. Where sources disagree or a fact comes from one source only, it
says so. Do not state anything marked "unconfirmed" as fact in the interview.

## 1. The story in two minutes

- **The founder.** Shah Ramezani, co-founder and CEO. Swiss, born and raised, studied
  business in Switzerland, started in banking, then moved to London. Former UBS analyst, and before Noah
  also worked at Lazard and Kingsway Capital and led the Asia division of The Hut Group
  (THG). He was 33 in June 2025. He started exploring crypto for payments in **2022**,
  first toying with Bitcoin, then deciding to build an API that lets developers move money
  with stablecoins.
- **The name.** "We're really building 'Noah's ark' to save everyone from the mass
  currency inflation." (Ramezani, Fortune, June 2025)
- **The Adyen co-founder.** Thijn Lamers joined Adyen in 2007 as **employee number 9**,
  stayed 11 years, ran global sales as Executive Vice President, and left after Adyen's
  2018 IPO. He then travelled, invested and sat on boards, and "missed building".
- **How they met.** Lamers met Ramezani in **March 2024**, first as a potential
  **investor**. He joined as **co-founder and President in June 2024** instead. Ramezani
  said investors paid "a Thijn premium".
- **Where it started: a consumer "money app" (confirmed).** In a 2023 investor Q&A
  (Concentric), NOAH was a consumer app built on bitcoin and stablecoins for emerging
  markets (Vietnam, Nigeria, the Philippines, India). It worked like a neobank with a
  WhatsApp-style interface to save, spend, send and receive money, with a dollar-stablecoin
  "checking account", reward points redeemable for bitcoin, a referral programme and a
  **400,000+ waitlist**, adding 5,000 to 10,000 users a week. The mission then was
  "financial freedom" for the 1.4 billion people without a bank.
- **The founding thesis.** Founded about four and a half years before January 2026
  (so around 2021). The bet: crypto's killer app would be **payments, not speculation**.
  The team saw people using stablecoin balances to move money, not to hold crypto.
- **The change of direction.** Noah became "API-first" infrastructure that sits under
  other companies. It first powered neobanks and wallets (**Jupiter, Ledger, KAST,
  Lemon**), then "recently shifted to an enterprise-first motion". Forbes (June 2026)
  says the turning point was realising that getting licensed in Europe was an advantage.
  It now does one thing only, cross-border payments, and does not "dabble" in others.
  *No source gives an exact date for the shift.*
- **The public launch.** June 2025, with a **$22m seed round** led by LocalGlobe. Lamers
  says Noah launched "a little over a year" after he met Ramezani.

## 1a. The shift: from consumer app to institutional infrastructure

### Three stages

| | **Stage 1: consumer app** | **Stage 2: powering other apps** | **Stage 3: enterprise and institutions** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **When** | ~2021 to 2023 | ~2023 to 2025 | 2025 onwards ("enterprise-first" by 2026) |
| **Customer** | Individuals in emerging markets (Vietnam, Nigeria, Philippines, India) | Consumer crypto apps and neobanks (Jupiter, Ledger, KAST, Lemon, MiniPay) | Payroll, payment companies, remittance firms, SMBs, enterprises, incumbents (Western Union) |
| **Product** | NOAH app: save, spend, send in dollars and bitcoin, WhatsApp-style, points, referrals | API that lets those apps offer accounts, conversion and payouts to their users | One API for pay-ins, conversion, payouts in 150+ markets, rules engine, treasury |
| **How it grew** | Referral programme, 400,000+ waitlist, 5,000 to 10,000 users a week | Each partner brings all its users at once | Enterprise sales, licences, partnerships (Lamers' Adyen playbook) |
| **Story told** | "Financial freedom", bitcoin as "the best currency on the planet" | Infrastructure behind the apps | "Invisible plumbing", settlement layer, cross-border payments only |

### Why, documented
1. **Licensing became the moat.** Forbes: Noah realised getting licensed in Europe was an
   advantage, so it sold "compliance and payment rails to businesses rather than chasing
   consumers" ("selling the shovels"). It now holds 40+ licences and approvals.
2. **Usage showed the real need.** The team saw stablecoin balances used to move money,
   not to speculate. The founding bet, "payments, not speculation", pointed to payments
   infrastructure.
3. **They built the missing layer.** Ramezani: Noah built the layer between stablecoins
   and regulated payments, "That's why we decided to be an infrastructure player."
4. **Value sits underneath.** "Everyone talks about the front end, the apps, the cards,
   the wallets." The network and settlement layer matter more.
5. **An enterprise sales leader joined.** Thijn Lamers (Adyen's global sales head) became
   co-founder in June 2024, a year before the "enterprise" story went public.

### Why, likely (my reading, not stated by Noah)
- **Distribution maths.** One partner like Ledger or Western Union brings millions of end
  users. A consumer app wins them one by one, at high cost.
- **Steadier revenue.** Usage-based fees on daily business flows beat consumer usage.
- **Rules favoured B2B.** Consumer crypto marketing got harder (e.g. UK crypto promotion
  rules from October 2023), while clear stablecoin rules (MiCA, the US GENIUS Act in July
  2025, Hong Kong in August 2025) made businesses comfortable adopting stablecoins.
- **The market validated infrastructure.** Stripe bought Bridge (Oct 2024) and
  Mastercard bought BVNK (2026), showing the value sits in B2B rails.
- **Crypto winter.** 2022 to 2023 was hard for consumer crypto apps.

### What stayed the same
- **Emerging markets and access.** From the unbanked app users to M-Pesa, PIX and NALA.
- **Dollars for people whose currency loses value.** "Noah's ark" against inflation.
- **Payments, not speculation.** The core belief never changed. Only who Noah serves did.

### What it means for marketing (your angle)
- **Stage 1 marketing** was consumer growth: referrals, waitlists, viral loops.
- **Stage 3 marketing** is B2B: credibility, case studies, pipeline, partnerships,
  account-based outreach, and making a technical product simple ("invisible plumbing").
- **You bridge both.** You grew a consumer crypto app (installs ~4x, AI UGC, retention)
  and generated B2B pipeline (20+ Institutional leads, 3 major clients). You understand
  the end users Noah's customers serve and the buyers Noah sells to.

**A question to ask them:** "I read Noah started as a consumer money app. What made you
decide to become infrastructure, and what did the consumer years teach you that still
shapes the product?"

## 1b. What the founders believe

**Shah Ramezani's core beliefs**
- **Money still crawls.** Cross-border payments run on rails built for another era.
  Each bank in a correspondent chain takes a fee and a day. Stablecoins settle in
  seconds.
- **Inflation and access.** In many emerging markets, local currencies lose value fast
  (he has cited Ghana's currency falling 70% in a year), so people cannot save or send
  money abroad. Dollars are a better store of value. His aim is to "put dollars in the
  hands of every user" and make remittances cheaper and faster.
- **Stablecoins are a payments story, not a crypto story.** He calls them "a settlement
  instrument", not a bet on price.
- **Make the technology invisible.** "A business shouldn't need to understand
  stablecoins to benefit from them." He compares it to TCP/IP and Voice over IP: nobody
  thinks about the plumbing.
- **The settlement layer matters most.** "Everyone talks about the front end, the apps,
  the cards, the wallets." Noah wants to own the layer underneath.
- **Do the hard, regulated work.** "The winners will be the ones who did the
  unglamorous, regulated work." That is why licences are central.
- **Focus.** One use case, cross-border payments, at global scale.

**Thijn Lamers' beliefs**
- "Having seen Adyen's growth firsthand, I recognise that stablecoins represent an even
  more profound shift."
- "We want to make stablecoin settlement invisible to the companies and individuals
  benefiting from it."
- "Everything is credibility." Storytelling should "create belief", not exaggerate.

## 1c. What they want Noah to become

- **The settlement layer for global money:** one stack and "a single execution fabric"
  that moves value anywhere, invisibly, for banks, fintechs and enterprises.
- **Incumbents on the rails, not just crypto-native firms.** Western Union is the proof.
- **AI agents paying each other.** Ramezani predicts AI agents will move money on their
  own, and Noah's 2026 outlook mentions agents paying through protocols such as x402.
- **Corporate treasury on-chain:** earning returns on idle balances (the Global Dollar
  Network product).
- **2026 theme from their year-end letter: "Rails will get real"**, moving from
  proving the idea to real volume. Plans include more licences, more products, more
  engineering and product hires, and bigger enterprise deals.

## 1e. How to use this in the interview

- **Their consumer roots are your strength.** Noah started as a consumer app that grew
  through referrals and a waitlist. You grew a consumer crypto app. You understand the
  end users their customers serve.
- **"Invisible stablecoins" is a marketing brief.** Their own line, "a business
  shouldn't need to understand stablecoins to benefit from them", is exactly the message
  you would build the website, case studies and ads around.
- **Emerging markets are personal for you.** You can speak to inflation, remittances and
  mobile money with real understanding.
- **AI agents.** Ramezani talks about agents moving money. You build with AI every day.



**One line:** payment infrastructure that connects bank transfers with stablecoins, so a
business can collect money in one currency and pay out in another, anywhere, in minutes.

How it works for a customer, through one API:
1. **Pay in.** The business (or its users) gets named virtual accounts in USD, EUR and
   GBP and receives money by ACH, Fedwire, SEPA, Faster Payments or SWIFT.
2. **Convert.** Noah converts to and from stablecoins (USDC, USDT, and others).
3. **Pay out.** Money goes out to 60+ currencies through local rails, for example
   **M-Pesa** (Kenya), **PIX** (Brazil) and **SPEI** (Mexico), or to crypto wallets.
4. **Automate.** A rules engine ("Orchestration") triggers payouts automatically.
5. **Compliance built in.** Noah runs the identity checks (KYC and KYB).

Noah is **not a consumer wallet app**. It runs the accounts and payouts behind other
companies' apps.

**Their positioning line:** "While others bolt stablecoins onto existing or broader
payment offerings, Noah is built around them." (Noah blog, June 2025)

## 3. Who uses it

- **Use cases:** payroll, remittances, marketplaces paying sellers, payment providers,
  neobanks, crypto exchanges, B2B trade, treasury.
- **Named customers and partners:**
  - **Western Union** (21 Oct 2026). Noah provides the onchain banking infrastructure for
    Western Union's Receiver Redirect, so people receiving a transfer can collect it as
    USDPT, a dollar stablecoin issued by Anchorage Digital Bank on Solana. Launch date,
    markets and fees not yet announced. **This is the biggest name to mention.**
  - **NALA** (Jan 2026): instant USD collection and real-time local payouts in emerging
    markets.
  - **Payd** (Feb 2026): stablecoin payments for African freelancers, settled in real
    time.
  - **Tenui** (Feb 2026): Latin American staffing company paying talent through Noah.
  - **Cedar**: B2B payments partnership in Africa.
  - **Ledger** (cash to stablecoin) and **MiniPay** (Opera's wallet).
  - **Circle** and **Paxos**: strategic alliances. **Global Dollar Network** (July 2026):
    Noah plugged its virtual accounts into Paxos's USDG network so enterprises can earn
    returns on idle balances ("reward-bearing enterprise treasuries").
  - **Solana Foundation** published a case study on Noah (April 2026).

## 4. Licences and regulation (their moat)

- **Europe:** on **30 Sep 2026** the Bank of Latvia authorised Noah's Riga entity (Noah LV
  SIA) as a **MiCA crypto-asset service provider** and a **payment institution**. That
  passports across 30 EEA countries and should give direct SEPA access. Noah says the PI
  licence took about one month and the MiCA licence about two.
- **US:** money transmitter licences. **Canada:** payment service provider registration.
- **Total:** "more than 40 licences, registrations and approvals". SOC 2 Type II audited.
