# Noah — company background for the in-person interview

Researched 18 Nov 2026. Where sources disagree or a fact comes from one source only, it
says so. Do not state anything marked "unconfirmed" as fact in the interview.

---

## 1. The story in two minutes

- **The founder.** Shah Ramezani, co-founder and CEO. Former UBS analyst, and before Noah
  also worked at Lazard and Kingsway Capital and led the Asia division of The Hut Group
  (THG). He was 33 in June 2025. He started exploring crypto for payments in **2022**,
  first toying with Bitcoin, then deciding to build an API that lets developers move money
  with stablecoins.
- **The name.** "We're really building 'Noah's ark' to save everyone from the mass
  currency inflation." (Ramezani, Fortune, June 2025)
- **The Adyen co-founder.** Thijn Lamers joined Adyen in 2007 as **employee number 9**,
  stayed 11 years, ran global sales as Executive Vice President, and left after Adyen's
  2018 IPO. He then travelled, invested and sat on boards, and "missed building".
- **How they met.** Lamers met Ramezani in **March 2024**, first as a potential
  **investor**. He joined as **co-founder and President in June 2024** instead. Ramezani
  said investors paid "a Thijn premium".
- **The change of direction.** A June 2026 Forbes piece ("In the stablecoin race, Noah is
  selling the shovels") says Noah repositioned from chasing consumers to selling
  compliance and payment rails to businesses, after realising that getting licensed in
  Europe was an advantage, not a burden. *I could not read the full article, so treat the
  detail as unconfirmed. Some company profiles list a 2020 founding date, which may refer
  to an earlier entity.*
- **The public launch.** June 2025, with a **$22m seed round** led by LocalGlobe. Lamers
  says Noah launched "a little over a year" after he met Ramezani.

## 2. What Noah does

**One line:** payment infrastructure that connects bank transfers with stablecoins, so a
business can collect money in one currency and pay out in another, anywhere, in minutes.

How it works for a customer, through one API:
1. **Pay in.** The business (or its users) gets named virtual accounts in USD, EUR and
   GBP and receives money by ACH, Fedwire, SEPA, Faster Payments or SWIFT.
2. **Convert.** Noah converts to and from stablecoins (USDC, USDT, and others).
3. **Pay out.** Money goes out to 60+ currencies through local rails, for example
   **M-Pesa** (Kenya), **PIX** (Brazil) and **SPEI** (Mexico), or to crypto wallets.
4. **Automate.** A rules engine ("Orchestration") triggers payouts automatically.
5. **Compliance built in.** Noah runs the identity checks (KYC and KYB).

Noah is **not a consumer wallet app**. It runs the accounts and payouts behind other
companies' apps.

**Their positioning line:** "While others bolt stablecoins onto existing or broader
payment offerings, Noah is built around them." (Noah blog, June 2025)

## 3. Who uses it

- **Use cases:** payroll, remittances, marketplaces paying sellers, payment providers,
  neobanks, crypto exchanges, B2B trade, treasury.
- **Named customers and partners:**
  - **Western Union** (21 Oct 2026). Noah provides the onchain banking infrastructure for
    Western Union's Receiver Redirect, so people receiving a transfer can collect it as
    USDPT, a dollar stablecoin issued by Anchorage Digital Bank on Solana. Launch date,
    markets and fees not yet announced. **This is the biggest name to mention.**
  - **NALA** (Jan 2026): instant USD collection and real-time local payouts in emerging
    markets.
  - **Payd** (Feb 2026): stablecoin payments for African freelancers, settled in real
    time.
  - **Tenui** (Feb 2026): Latin American staffing company paying talent through Noah.
  - **Cedar**: B2B payments partnership in Africa.
  - **Ledger** (cash to stablecoin) and **MiniPay** (Opera's wallet).
  - **Circle** and **Paxos**: strategic alliances. **Global Dollar Network** (July 2026):
    Noah plugged its virtual accounts into Paxos's USDG network so enterprises can earn
    returns on idle balances ("reward-bearing enterprise treasuries").
  - **Solana Foundation** published a case study on Noah (April 2026).

## 4. Licences and regulation (their moat)

- **Europe:** on **30 Sep 2026** the Bank of Latvia authorised Noah's Riga entity (Noah LV
  SIA) as a **MiCA crypto-asset service provider** and a **payment institution**. That
  passports across 30 EEA countries and should give direct SEPA access. Noah says the PI
  licence took about one month and the MiCA licence about two.
- **US:** money transmitter licences. **Canada:** payment service provider registration.
- **Total:** "more than 40 licences, registrations and approvals". SOC 2 Type II audited.

## 6. Competitors (from earlier research)

- **Bridge** (Stripe): developer-first, issuing stablecoins, Visa cards.
- **BVNK** (Mastercard): enterprise payment companies. **Do not mention you applied there.**
- **Ripple** (with Rail): banks, its own token.

**Noah's edge:** independent, stablecoin-native, the widest payout coverage on local
rails, and a fast licensing machine. Now with Western Union as proof of trust.
