# Plasma — Growth / Paid Acquisition: hiring-manager read

London or relocate. £100K – £150K plus token compensation.

## What the JD actually screens for

The posting is unusually revealing. Decoded:

- **"You inherit a live product with real spend"** plus **no big team behind you** — they
  need someone who takes over an existing account on day one. Zero ramp-up tolerance.
- **"Take us beyond our crypto-native audience"** — this is the real brief. They have
  exhausted crypto Twitter and need mainstream consumer acquisition.
- **"Meta, Google, TikTok and YouTube"** — note the order. Apple Search Ads appears once,
  mid-list. Her strongest verified achievement is in the channel they care least about.
- **"attribution, incrementality and cohort analysis, and where each one can mislead
  you"** — the most sophisticated line in the document. They want someone who understands
  confounding and validity, not someone who reads a dashboard.
- **"people who fund and keep spending"** — they measure funded accounts and retention.
  Her headline number is installs. The first question a hiring manager asks is whether
  those 562 installs funded anything, and the CV currently cannot answer it.

## Fit

A genuine stretch. Strong on structured testing, multi-market work, full-funnel range,
creative velocity and zero-to-one building. She matches the hard parts — "proof, not
claims" and structured creative testing — better than most mid-level applicants will.

Gaps that would get probed: roughly two years of commercial experience against a
£100–150K band; £50k/month budget scale versus what Plasma will deploy; no AppsFlyer or
any MMP; no Meta AEM; no data warehouse work.

On seniority she is under the bar as written. What gets her a conversation anyway is the
evidence quality, not the years.

## Note on the psychology background

The standing preference is to keep psychology as an education credential only. For this
JD specifically that is worth partially reversing. "Incrementality" and "where each one
can mislead you" is a request for experimental rigour, and an MRes in Experimental
Psychology with Data Science plus 128 controlled campaign tests is a coherent,
differentiated story. It is also the best available counter to the experience gap.

## What belongs in the cover letter, not the CV

- Leaving a permanent analyst role at Malaberg to take a 6-month internship. On paper it
  is a step down in title and security, and it is the genuinely unusual move in her
  history. Framed as optimising for category experience and ownership it is a strength;
  left unexplained it is what a hiring manager wonders about.
- The fixed term ending, which is why a 6-month tenure is not a red flag.
- The AppsFlyer/MMP, Meta AEM and data-warehouse gaps. Do not fake them on the CV.
