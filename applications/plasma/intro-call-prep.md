# Plasma intro call: prep sheet

Growth / Paid Acquisition. London. £100K – £150K plus tokens.

An intro call is a 20–30 minute screen. It checks four things: can you explain yourself
clearly, do you actually want Plasma, are there any obvious red flags, and do the
logistics work (salary, start date, right to work). It is not a technical deep dive. Keep
answers to 60–90 seconds and let them ask follow-ups.

Spoken answers below are written in your voice. Say them in your own words. Do not read
them out.

---

## 1. Who you are likely talking to

- **A recruiter or talent partner.** Expect motivation, logistics and salary.
- **Nathan Lenga, Head of Growth**, probably your manager. Expect more on channels,
  testing and numbers. Look at his LinkedIn and X before the call. Note what he has posted
  about recently and use it once.
- Paul Faecks, CEO, is unlikely at this stage. If he is on, lead with retention and card
  spend. That is what he posts about.

---

## 2. "Tell me about yourself" (60 seconds)

> I am a growth marketer working on paid acquisition for consumer finance apps. Right now
> I am at Blockchain.com, where I run paid across five channels and 26 markets. Before
> that I was a performance marketer at Malaberg, managing over £50k a month in paid spend
> on a regulated consumer finance product. My background is an MRes in Experimental
> Psychology with Data Science, so I approach growth as testing. At Blockchain.com I ran
> 128 structured tests, scaled daily installs from 187 to 562 with CPI held flat, and
> built the AI UGC workflow the team now uses for paid social creative. I like to do the
> work myself. Set up the account, write the brief, run the test, cut what does not work.

Why this works: profession first, scope, one proof, the "hands-on" signal that an early
growth hire needs.

---

## 3. "Why Plasma?" (short version)

The full answer is in `answer-why-plasma.md`. For a call, cut it to this:

> Three reasons. First, the product removes steps. Moving stablecoins usually means going
> from an exchange to a wallet to a card app, and every step is a place users drop off.
> Plasma puts it on one chain, and Binance already withdraws USDT straight to Plasma.
> Second, timing. New users coming from TradFi will not want to move money between
> wallets. They will pick one place and stay. Third, Plasma One is new, so there is no
> playbook yet. I would rather set the baseline than inherit one.

If they push on what makes Plasma different from other stablecoin chains:

> Tempo and Arc sell infrastructure to businesses. Plasma also runs its own consumer app,
> so the growth team works directly with the people who hold and spend the money.

## 6. Hard questions and how to handle them

**"You have about two years of experience. This role is senior."**

> That is fair. What I bring is range and that I build things myself. At Blockchain.com I
> ran paid across five channels and 26 markets and built the creative and reporting
> workflows the team uses. For an early growth hire I think that matters more than having
> managed a big budget inside an existing structure.

**"Did those installs turn into funded users?"**

This is the question the JD is built around ("people who fund and keep spending").
If you have any funded-rate, first-deposit or retention number, use it here.
If you do not:

> My target metric was installs at a fixed CPI, and funded-account data sat with another
> team. At Plasma I would optimise to first deposit and first card spend, not installs.
> Card spend is the number that matters for Plasma One.

Do not invent a number. Saying what you would measure is a good answer.

**"Have you used AppsFlyer or another MMP? Meta AEM?"**

> Not hands-on. I have worked with Apple Search Ads attribution and conversion tracking
> setup. I know the gap, and an MMP is something I can learn quickly. I understand the
> measurement side from my research background, including where attribution misleads you
> and when you need an incrementality test instead.

**"Your strongest numbers are in Apple Search Ads, but we care about Meta and TikTok."**

> The paid search numbers are the biggest, but the paid social work is where I moved
> things the most. CPI from $25 to $6 on TikTok and Google video. Plasma has no active
> ads on Meta that I could find, so the first job is creative supply, and that is what I
> built at Blockchain.com.

Remember: the Meta Ads Library only shows active ads. Say "I could not find any active
ads", never "you do not run paid social".

**"What would you do in your first 90 days?"**

> First month, learn the funnel. Where people drop off between install, first deposit and
> first card spend, and what is already tracked. Second month, build creative supply and
> start structured tests on Meta and TikTok in two or three markets. Third month, scale
> what works and cut what does not, measured on funded users and spend.

**"Which markets would you go after?"**

> Plasma's own messaging is about access to dollars, not crypto. So I would start where
> people want dollars and cheap cross-border money. Turkey, Latin America, the Middle
> East. I also speak Mandarin, Cantonese and Malay, which helps for Southeast Asia.

## 7. Logistics

- **Salary.** The band is £100K – £150K plus tokens. Let them name a number first if you
  can. If pushed: "I have seen the posted range and I am comfortable within it. I would
  like to understand the token part before I give an exact number."
- **Start date.** Your fixed term ends November 2026, so you can start soon. Know your
  exact available date.
- **Right to work.** Have a clear, accurate one-line answer ready.
- **Location.** London. Ask about office days.

---

## 8. Questions to ask them

Pick three. The first two are the best ones.

1. "Is the first deposit where you see the biggest drop-off?"
2. "Who owns attribution today, and what is already set up?"
3. "Which markets does the Stripe card launch first in?"
4. "What does the growth team look like in twelve months? Would I be building it, or will
   someone be hired above me?"
5. "What does success look like for this role after six months?"

---

## 9. Do not raise

- XPL's price. It is down more than 80% from its peak.
- Falling stablecoin balances on the chain (about $6.35B to $1.31B over the past year).
- "Zero-fee transfers" as a blanket claim. Say "Plasma One charges nothing to receive,
  even from other networks", or use their own line "Send money across borders for free".
- Yield as a fixed number. It changes. Say "up to".

If they bring up XPL, be calm and brief: you are aware of it, Paul has addressed the team
tokens being locked, and you are focused on product usage.
