# Plasma founder watch

Updated 2 Nov 2026 from Paul Faecks's public X posts and a web research run.

## Founder

**Paul Faecks**, founder and CEO, X [@pauliepunt](https://x.com/pauliepunt) (18.6K
followers). Ex professional poker player, Deribit Insights researcher, co-founder of
Alloy (Berlin). Founded Plasma in 2024.

Christian Angermayer is listed as a co-founder by Tracxn, but most sources name Paul
alone. Treat Paul as the founder in conversation.

Leadership: CTO Hans Walter Behrens, COO "Lucid", Chief Strategy Officer Zaheer
Ebtikar, Head of Ecosystem Vincent Rong, **Head of Growth Nathan Lenga** (likely the
hiring line for a growth role).

## Themes

1. **Retention and loving the product** over incentive-driven growth. "Hype follows
   quality."
2. **Speed to scale**, measured in card spend.
3. **Distribution through partners**: Stripe, Deel, Whop.
4. **Markets**: cross-border and emerging markets (Turkey, Middle East, South America,
   Argentina, Nigeria, Brazil). USDT first.
5. **Competition** named in his podcasts: Western Union on distribution, Stripe's Tempo
   chain.
