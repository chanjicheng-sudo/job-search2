## Themes

1. **Retention over acquisition.** His most recent post. He measures spenders kept, not
   sign-ups.
2. **Speed and scale.** "Fastest to one billion". $100M card spend is the milestone he
   celebrates.
3. **Distribution through partners.** Deel (payroll in stablecoins) and Whop (creator
   payouts). Getting into the apps where people already get paid.
4. **Card spend is the headline metric**, more than trading or deposits.
