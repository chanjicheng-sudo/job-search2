Compiled 2 November 2026, including the research agent's findings.

## 1. The founder

  and crypto researcher. Co-founded Alloy in Berlin, then founded Plasma in 2024.

  name Paul alone. Treat Paul as the founder.

  up before the call.

## 4. How Plasma One works

It is a hybrid of DeFi and CeFi.

- **DeFi side:** the wallet is self-custodial, so only the user holds the keys. Yield
  comes from an on-chain vault run by Veda.
- **CeFi side:** the Visa card is issued through Rain, and Bridge is reported to provide
  fiat access. Plasma does not hold a bank licence.
- **Deposits from other chains** cost nothing on Plasma One's side. The app shows BSC
  deposits with "Fees: Zero". The sender still pays their own chain's network fee.

**Safe phrasing**

- "A self-custodial stablecoin app with a card and fiat access through regulated partners."
- "Plasma One charges nothing to receive, even from other networks."

## 5. Key points for the interviews

- **Lead with retention.** Paul's latest post is about it, and the perps story fits
  exactly: she found why users churned, fixed it, and retention went up 12%.
- **Talk about users who spend, not installs.** Card spend is the number he celebrates.
- **Mention partner-led growth.** The publisher outreach and the institutional leads she
  generated show she can do it.

**Questions to ask**

- "Is the first deposit where you see the biggest drop-off?"
- "Which markets does the Stripe card launch first in?"

**Don't raise**

- XPL's price. It is down more than 80% from its peak.
- Falling stablecoin balances on the chain, from about $6.35B to $1.31B over the past year.
- "Zero-fee transfers" as a blanket claim.
