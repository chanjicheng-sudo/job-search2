# Casual DM to the Plasma CEO

Intended to be screenshotted and sent. Her text, one typo fixed, nothing else touched.

> I think Plasma has great potential bc the main selling point is around hassle free
> stablecoin payments which fits in the tradfi getting into crypto movement plus zero gas
> fee can be appealing to the target audience (marketing should promote this on paid
> channels). One issue is that the paid and organic presence of the company is relatively
> weak. The tiktok posts look like meta ads but I couldn't find any of their ads on meta.
>
> Judging from their posts I think the company is not against ai created assets, so I'd
> really approach the growth with pushing AI UGCs across Google, meta, tiktok etc, since
> these channels are the 'less crypto native' people that they'd like to acquire.

## Why this version works

- **"The TikTok posts look like Meta ads" is the sharpest line.** It says she actually
  watched the content and understood why repurposed ad creative dies on an organic-native
  channel.
- **Inferring from those posts that they are not precious about AI assets** is exactly
  the kind of reasoning the job is. She read a constraint off observable evidence.
- **"(marketing should promote this on paid channels)" resolves the main objection.**
  Without "on paid channels" it would have told the CEO to promote free transfers, which
  he already does prominently on his own homepage — the one kind of mistake that makes a
  reader discount everything else. Scoped to paid, it becomes the real observation: the
  hook is live on the website and nowhere else, because there are no ads.

## Changes made along the way

- **"Spamming" was cut.** To a CEO of a regulated financial product it sounds like brand
  damage and low-quality volume. The instinct — high volume, cheap, let the channel sort
  it out — is right, so the substance stayed and the word went.
- Her analysis originally had no sender and no ask. Both still need to live in the DM
  around the screenshot.

## Still missing

1. **His name.** "Hey" alone wastes the one line that proves this is not a mass DM.
2. **An ask he can say yes to in four words.** Not "hire me" — sending the rest, or
   fifteen minutes.
3. **The assets.** The strongest version of this is not a message describing what she
   would make. It is two or three actual Plasma ads with the message as the caption. At
   ~$5 a video that is affordable, and it inverts the dynamic: not telling him what to
   do, showing him something that already exists. Words alone compete with everyone else
   in his DMs. Assets do not.

## Optional, worth considering

- **"Zero gas fee" is the crypto-native way to say it.** Plasma's own site says "free
  transfers" and "send money across borders for free". Since her argument is that the
  target is *less* crypto native, using their consumer phrasing would quietly prove the
  point she is making. The kind of thing a founder notices.
- The first sentence runs long for a screenshot. A full stop after "movement" helps
  legibility on a phone without changing how it sounds.

## Risks

- Going around a live application can irritate the hiring manager. At an early-stage
  company initiative is usually the point, so this is a small risk.
- A compliance note on the free-transfer claim: it covers simple USDT transfers on Plasma
  routes. Use their brand marks only in something sent privately, never posted publicly.
