## Why would you like to work at Emma?

I want to build something from the ground up, and own the result. At Blockchain.com I have grown a consumer finance app across 26 markets, but most of that growth was paid. Emma wants to grow organically, through the app store, referrals, partnerships and content. That is the harder and more valuable kind of growth, and it is what I want to get good at. I like that Emma is built on product and data. I work the same way. I research users, test ideas fast and keep what works. Your mission also matters to me. Most people do not have a clear view of their money, and Emma gives them one.
