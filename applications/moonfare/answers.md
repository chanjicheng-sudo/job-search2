## Why are you interested in this role at Moonfare?

I am interested because this role sits where marketing meets investors, and every send has to be right. Moonfare works across many regions, and each one has its own compliance rules and its own kind of investor. At Blockchain.com, we saw that users in Africa tend to take on more risk, while users in the UK are more balanced, so we had to create different content for each region. I enjoy that kind of challenge, where one size does not fit all.

I also like that the role covers the whole journey from first interest to the handover to Investor Relations. I have worked with our Wealth and Institutional teams, brought in 3 major high net worth clients, and handed 20+ active leads to business development, so I understand what a good handover looks like.

## Tell us about a time you proactively identified an opportunity to use AI or automation to solve a business problem.

Our ads relied on real creators. Their videos were expensive, and once they ran out we had nothing new to test. On TikTok an ad only lasts about two weeks. I noticed competitors were already running AI-generated UGC ads, so I proposed we build our own.

I built the workflow in 2 weeks, covering everything from prompt generation and avatar creation to the final video. The early output kept failing in the same ways, so I spent time on prompt engineering until the videos were good enough to run. I reviewed every asset against our brand and compliance standards before it went live.

It cut production time by 75%, lifted click-through rate from 0.4% to 1.15%, and cut cost per install from $25 to $6. When we relaunched our yield product, the videos drove 600+ installs in the first 7 days.

What I learned is that AI output can look right and still be wrong. The tool saves time, but someone still has to check every piece against the brand and the rules. I now build that check into anything I automate.
