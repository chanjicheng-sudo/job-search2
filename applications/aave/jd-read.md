# Aave Labs — Growth Marketer: hiring-manager read

Marketing team, reports to the SVP of Marketing. No location or band in the posting.
Aave Labs is London-headquartered, but confirm the req before investing in it.

## What the product is

The consumer product this role grows is the **Aave App**, a mobile savings app that takes
deposits from a bank account or in stablecoins and pays a yield on them. Announced in
November 2025, launched on iOS first, Android expected Q3 2026, with card, swap and FX
features to be phased in during 2026 pending regulatory clearance. Since July 2026 the
same savings technology has also been offered to fintechs as a B2B product. (Sources:
aave.com blog, governance.aave.com ARFC "Aave App Launch", The Block, The Defiant.
Reported yields and dates vary between outlets, so do not quote a rate in an
application.)

That changes the fit. This is a **regulated, iOS-first, stablecoin consumer savings
app in staged rollout across markets**, which is close to a description of what she has
done for the last six months.

## What the JD actually screens for

- **"high-growth consumer apps - like a fintech disruptor or a gaming product with
  millions of users"**: they want app-growth people, not DeFi-native marketers. The
  crypto background helps, but the screen is consumer app growth.
- **The channel list is broad**: "social, search, lifecycle, referrals, and
  partnerships". This is a generalist growth seat, not a paid-media specialist. Range
  wins over depth.
- **"funnels, landing pages, onboarding flows, and engagement campaigns"**, together with
  "acquisition, activation, and retention": they care about what happens after the
  install. The same gap as Plasma.
- **"keep, kill, or scale"**: a test-velocity culture. 128 tests and cutting 26 campaigns
  answers this word for word.
- **"3–5 years"**, followed by an explicit invitation to apply anyway.

## Fit

**Strong on substance, short on years.** This is a closer match than Plasma on product
and a lower bar on seniority. A "Growth Marketer" title with no "Senior" and 3–5 years
puts it a band below Plasma's £100–150K.

| What they ask for | What she has |
|---|---|
| Acquisition at a high-growth consumer app | 187 to 562 daily installs at flat CPI, 2 to 26 countries, on a crypto app |
| Experiments across paid and organic | 128 structured tests, 20+ Custom Product Pages, MRes in experimental design |
| Landing pages and funnels | Malaberg: 10 to 50+ daily conversions (5x) from rebuilt landing pages |
| Paid social | AI UGC workflow: paid social CPI $25 to $6, CTR 0.4% to 1.15% |
| Lifecycle | CRM localisation, +10% email open rate |
| Referrals and partnerships | 80-publisher channel that sourced 3 Wealth clients. Referral and KOL channels in the Malaysia work, in flight |
| Owning KPIs and reporting | Automated reporting at both employers, Streamlit dashboard |
| Collaborating with Product, Engineering, Creative | Front-End, Data Engineering, AI Engineering and Security, plus 3 external partners |
| On-brand, regulated touchpoints | Regulated messaging review in two roles, Earn pages across jurisdictions |

**The iOS point is the strongest single argument.** For Plasma, Apple Search Ads was the
channel they cared least about. For an app that launched on iOS first, App Store search
and product-page conversion are the front door. Scaling Apple Search Ads to 26 countries
at a flat CPI is directly relevant here.

The Malaysia loans work is also unusually on-topic. Aave is a lending protocol, and the
bullet names referral and KOL channels, which the JD lists.

## Gaps that would get probed

1. **Activation and retention.** The highest-value missing figure in the repository,
   again. Everything she can prove ends at the install. "Activation" and "retention"
   appear in the first bullet of the role. Any funded-account, first-deposit or D30
   figure behind the 3x install growth would change this application more than anything
   else. See OPEN-QUESTIONS.md item 1.
2. **Years.** About 21 months of commercial experience (Feb 2025 onwards) against 3–5 years. The JD's own "apply
   anyway" line helps. The evidence density does the rest.
3. **Referral programme.** Named twice in the JD. She has not run one. The Malaysia work
   covers referral channels but has no outcome yet. Do not overstate it.
4. **Onboarding flows.** She has tested App Store pages and landing pages, not in-app
   onboarding. Say so if asked. The landing-page work is the nearest evidence.
5. **Product analytics.** "Fluent in analytics tools" in an app context usually means
   Amplitude or Mixpanel. Unconfirmed, so not on the CV. Power BI, Clarity, SQL and
   Python are stated instead.
6. **No MMP.** Same as Plasma. Do not claim AppsFlyer.

## What belongs in the cover letter, not the CV

- **Why Aave.** A stablecoin savings app trying to reach people outside crypto is the
  thing she has been doing at Blockchain.com: regulated consumer messaging, multi-market
  rollout, iOS acquisition. One concrete line about the Aave App, not the protocol.
- **The fixed term.** The internship ends this month. Show it as completed, not
  abandoned.
- **The years gap**, stated once and answered with the test volume.

## Questions worth asking them

- What is the activation event for the Aave App, first deposit or something else, and
  is it instrumented by market?
- Which markets is the app live in, and is the growth plan market by market?
- Does this role own paid budget directly, or run experiments on a budget someone else
  holds?
