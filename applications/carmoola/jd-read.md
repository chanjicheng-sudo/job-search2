# Carmoola — Growth Manager: hiring-manager read

**Confidential.** Pivotal London (Nusrat) asked that the role and its details are not
shared. Keep this file out of anything sent to anyone else.

**A real fit on the work, a stretch on years, and the best-paid live process.** First
stage was booked through Pivotal for Mon 23 Nov 2026, 16:30, with Kate Madden (Chief of
Staff).

## What the role is

- **Carmoola** is an FCA-regulated direct car finance lender that runs through an app.
  Buyers get a budget from a soft credit check in about 60 seconds, then shop at any UK
  dealer or private seller and pay with a virtual card (Apple Pay or Google Pay).
  Hire purchase over 1 to 5 years. 20,000+ customers, #66 on the 2026 Sifted 100, Series
  A led by QED.
- **The job:** own Meta end to end (structure, budgets, scaling, kill rules), be the
  in-house Google expert overseeing their agency, and run the landing page A/B testing
  programme. Weekly performance report. Works with a creative strategist. Reports to the
  Head of Growth (Sophie Harris).
- **The path:** months 0–3 own Meta and landing pages, months 3–6 own the report and the
  test roadmap, beyond 6 months bring paid search in-house and move into CRM and new
  channels.
- **Pay:** the JD says £60–70k. Nusrat's email said £50–60k. Settle this with Nusrat
  before talking numbers with Carmoola.

## Why it is a stretch

- **Years.** They ask for 3–5 years, mostly in-house. She has roughly two: Malaberg
  (Feb 2025 – Apr 2026) and Blockchain.com (from May 2026, fixed term, intern title). The
  JD softens this ("we care more about how fast you learn"), so the answer is speed:
  2 to 26 countries and 128 tests in six months.
- **Google Ads depth.** Her strongest search numbers are Apple Search Ads, not Google.
  They want someone who can challenge a Google agency on day one. Say this plainly and
  lean on the testing discipline, which transfers.
- **Meta depth is unproven on paper.** Meta is the first line of the job. Her headline
  paid social numbers come from TikTok and Google. Confirm which channels the Malaberg
  £50k+ budget ran on before the second round.
- **Their tools.** Funnel.io, Hex and Foresight. She has used none, but has built her own
  automated reporting twice.

## The story

Her narrative has been "I moved into crypto for the exposure". That does not work here,
and it does not need to. The real thread is **marketing financial products to ordinary
people, where trust decides the sale**: Malaberg (consumer finance), Blockchain.com
(crypto), Carmoola (consumer lending). Crypto was one chapter.

What she can honestly sell, since cars themselves do not excite her:

1. Ownership: Meta and landing pages from day one, with a path to all paid channels.
2. Landing pages are where her best result came from.
3. An AI-first team that works the way she does.
4. A consumer app in regulated finance.
5. A good manager: Nusrat knows the Head of Growth and vouches for her.

## Recommendation

Worth pursuing hard. It is the closest match to the work she does best (paid plus
landing pages plus testing), it has the clearest ownership of any live process, and the
JD band is the highest. The years gap is real but the JD invites candidates who learn
fast. Do not fake interest in cars. Show interest through specifics instead: having tried
the eligibility check, the free valuation tool as a way in, and the attribution-lag
question below.

## Smart points to make

- **Optimise for funded loans, not sign-ups.** Send approved and funded loans back to
  Meta (Conversions API) and Google (offline conversions) so the platforms learn what a
  good customer is.
- **Attribution lag.** Someone checks a budget, then shops for weeks. Meta's default
  7-day window probably undervalues paid media. Ask how they measure it.
- **The free valuation tool** is a way in from search: people valuing their car are
  about to buy the next one. Ties to the SEO/GEO bonus point.
- **Channels:** the app directly, Zuto (Carmoola is one of the lenders on its broker
  marketplace) and a dealer programme. Ask how growth splits across them.

Do not quote their CPA, Trustpilot ratings, or "never pays commission". None are
verified.
