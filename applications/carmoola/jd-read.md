## Carmoola versus a bank loan

For a used-car buyer with good credit, the real alternative is a bank personal loan.

**Where Carmoola wins**
- Built around buying a car: budget, car, seller paid and loan managed in one app.
- Budget before shopping, from a soft check that does not hit the credit score.
- Protection if the car is faulty. Under hire purchase the finance company supplies the
  car, so the buyer generally has rights against it. A bank loan gives none of this,
  least of all on a private sale.
- The car is checked before the money is released.
- The seller is paid directly, so no lump sum sits with the buyer to be scammed out of.
- Secured on the car, so it can suit people a bank would decline for unsecured credit.

**Where a bank wins**
- Often cheaper for strong credit. Carmoola advertises "from 6.9% APR", representative
  13.9% APR.
- The buyer owns the car at once and can sell it without settling a loan.
- The money can go on anything (repairs, insurance).

One line: "A bank gives you money. Carmoola gives you a way to buy a car."

## Fraud and fake sellers

Carmoola does not publish its exact checks. How a lender like this usually protects
itself and the buyer:

- **Vehicle history check** before lending: outstanding finance, stolen, written off,
  cloned plates. Reviewers mention a car check. Coverage not confirmed.
- **Proof of ownership** on a private sale: seller matches the registered keeper on the
  V5C logbook.
- **Money goes lender to seller**, never through the buyer.
- **If fraud slips through,** under hire purchase the lender bought the car, so the loss
  generally sits with the lender, not the buyer. General understanding of hire purchase,
  not legal advice and not a confirmed Carmoola policy.

Why it matters to growth: private sales are a selling point and a fraud cost. Nusrat's
email says Carmoola uses machine learning for fraud prevention. Paid media should not
pull in risky traffic, so growth and fraud have to work together. Raise this with the
Head of Growth in a later round, not with Kate.
