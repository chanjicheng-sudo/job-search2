Dear McKinsey recruiting team,

I am a growth marketer at Blockchain.com, and my strength is problem solving. I look past the obvious answer to find the real pain point, then use data and AI to build a solution the team has not tried before. That is what I want to bring to McKinsey's clients.

This year, I left a full-time job for an internship. I wanted to break into financial services and digital assets, and Blockchain.com was my way in.

As an intern, I did not run one channel. I worked across Retail, Wealth and Institutional, and each team had a different problem. Users were leaving a product. A market had never been tested. Our ads could not be produced fast enough. Each time, I had to find the real pain point, build a solution with Product, Legal or Engineering, and prove it worked. Those projects were the most rewarding part of my year, more than optimising one channel day to day. Strategy consulting is that work, one problem after another, which is why I am applying to your Growth, Marketing & Sales Practice.

The first thing I learnt is that the real problem is rarely the obvious one. Users were leaving our perps product. Before fixing anything, I surveyed 114 active, inactive and churned users, and found that many did not understand how perps work or what the risks are. We made the explainer always visible and reworked the help page. Retention grew by 12%.

The second is that a resource problem can often be solved with AI. Our ads relied on real creators, who were expensive and ran out fast. I saw competitors using AI-generated videos, proposed we build our own, and built the workflow in 2 weeks. Production time fell by 75%, click-through rate nearly tripled, and cost per install dropped from $25 to $6. Then I built a skill that writes the prompts, so the team could run it without me.

The third is that I do my best work when I own a project end to end. Our loans product had never been marketed in Malaysia. On my own initiative, I researched the local rules, set up a compliance review with Legal, and chose the partners to launch with. We converted 4 high net worth clients in the first month.

Consulting gives me more of that. A new problem, a new industry, and a small team that has to find the pain point fast. That is the work I want to do.

Kind regards,

Jessie Chan
