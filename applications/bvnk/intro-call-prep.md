## 5. Stories to have ready (only these numbers)

**Product adoption (perps).** Users were leaving our perps product. I ran research with
active, inactive and churned users (114 responses across 3 segments) and found many did
not understand how perps work or the risks. We made the product and risk explainer always
visible and reworked the help page. Retention grew by 12%.
- **Careful on volume.** Your letter says you grew perps volume 5x. Say it this way
  instead: "Over the same two months, cumulative trading volume grew from $100m to $500m."
  The research fed into that growth, but did not cause it on its own.

**Go-to-market (Malaysia).** I led the launch of our loans product in Malaysia on my own
initiative. I researched local regulation, worked with Legal on tone and risk
disclosures, set up a new compliance review process, and ran KOL, affiliate and referral
partners. We converted 4 high net worth clients in the first month.
- **Careful on the licence line.** Your letter says Blockchain.com "did not have the
  official license to operate". Do not repeat or expand on it. If they ask, say: "It was a
  new market, so we worked closely with Legal to set the tone and risk disclosures, and
  built a compliance review process before launch." Then move on.

**B2B and clients.** During MiCA, a major exchange was losing access to European clients.
I spotted it, built an outreach list of 80 crypto publishers with AI, and it brought in 3
major high net worth clients. For Institutional, a 100-operator prospect database gave BD
20+ active leads. I work with our Senior B2B Product Marketing Manager on Institutional
and Wealth projects.

**AI and the rebrand.** When we rebranded our yield product, every creator video carried
the old name. I built our first in-house AI UGC workflow in 2 weeks and recreated them.
Production time −75%, CTR 0.4% to 1.15%, 600+ installs in the first 7 days. I built a
skill that writes the prompts and handed it to the team.

**Compliant communications.** I supported the rollout of the UK FinProm approval process
with Compliance, reviewing regulated messaging across all retail products in the UK, US
and EU. The JD asks for communications that stay clear, consistent and compliant with
Legal and Compliance. This is your closest match, so use it.

**CRM.** API tools that localise Iterable emails into 24 languages, open rate +10%.

## 6. The hard questions, and what to say

**"This role is quite senior. How does your experience fit?"**
Do not apologise. Point to scope, then ownership.
> I am early in my career, but the work I have done is product marketing work. I have
> launched in a new market, turned customer research into a product change, and worked
> with Legal and Compliance on regulated messaging across three regions. I also work with
> our Senior B2B Product Marketing Manager on Institutional projects. I learn fast, and AI
> lets me do the work of a bigger team.

If the recruiter says the level is too senior, ask: "Is there another role in the team
that would be a better fit?" The JD says "you may be a great fit for this role or another
role in our team". Ask about associate or growth product marketing roles.

**"Have you done sales enablement?"**
> Not as a formal function. The nearest is my Institutional work, where I built the
> prospect database that gave BD 20+ active leads, and briefing partners with 50+ assets
> across markets. I would like to build enablement properly, with battlecards, case
> studies and win/loss notes.

**"Have you worked with stablecoins?"**
Do not claim stablecoin product work. Say what you know: Blockchain.com supports
stablecoins, you understand on- and off-ramps, MiCA, and why businesses use stablecoins
for cross-border payouts. Know the basics: stablecoin pre-funding, settlement,
on-ramp and off-ramp, embedded wallets.

**"Why are you leaving Blockchain.com?"**
> My internship is a six-month fixed term, so it is ending. I want a permanent role where I
> can own product marketing.

**"Are you interviewing elsewhere?"**
Yes, without names. **Do not mention Noah**, which competes with BVNK.
