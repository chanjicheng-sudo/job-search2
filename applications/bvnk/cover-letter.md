Dear BVNK hiring team,

I build MCP and API integrations and internal bots to remove repeat work. I have not used n8n yet. The workflows it runs are the kind I already build in code.

I have not built a sales enablement programme before. That part of the role would be new to me. I have worked closely with our B2B product marketing team on Institutional and Wealth projects, so I know how that work is put together. From the start I can bring customer research, compliant messaging and automation.

I want to work on stablecoin infrastructure. Stablecoins are the part of crypto that businesses use to move money. BVNK makes them work inside the systems those businesses already run. That is the problem I want to market.

I can share references from product marketing, compliance and legal colleagues at Blockchain.com.
