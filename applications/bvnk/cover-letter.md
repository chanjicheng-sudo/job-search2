Dear BVNK hiring team,

I am applying for the Product Marketing Manager role in London.

I am a growth marketer at Blockchain.com, working across Retail, Wealth and Institutional. Most of my work is go-to-market, customer communications and product adoption, in a regulated environment.

On product adoption, I grew cumulative perps trading volume 5x, from $100m to $500m in two months. It brought in 3 major clients. For Institutional, I built a database of 100 operators that gave our business development team 20+ active leads. I work closely with our B2B product marketing team on Institutional and Wealth projects.

On customer communications, I localised our CRM communications into 24 languages with API tools. Email open rates went up 10%. I also helped our Compliance team roll out the UK FinProm approval process.

On systems, I automated our daily performance reporting with Claude Code. I led its launch as a company-wide dashboard with Data Engineering and Security. It saves the team about 10 hours a month. I also built the company's first in-house AI content workflow, which cut production time by 75%.

I want to join BVNK because it makes stablecoins usable inside the systems businesses already run. Now that it is part of Mastercard, that infrastructure can reach a network of over 200 countries. I see two markets where this matters most. The first is Europe. MiCAR has given businesses clear rules, and BVNK already holds a MiCA licence and direct access to SEPA. I saw during the MiCAR transition how clear rules push clients towards licensed providers. That is BVNK's position in Europe. The second is Southeast Asia. Businesses there pay suppliers, staff and partners across many currencies, and cross-border payments are still slow and expensive. Stablecoin payouts and settlement solve exactly that problem. Hong Kong and Singapore now have stablecoin rules, which gives enterprises the confidence to start. I know this region well. I have taken a product to market in Malaysia, and I speak Mandarin, Cantonese and Malay. I would like to help BVNK grow there.

I can share references from product marketing, compliance and legal colleagues at Blockchain.com.

Thank you for reading.

Jessie Chan
