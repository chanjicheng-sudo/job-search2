I work closely with our B2B product marketing team on Institutional and Wealth projects.

I drove 600+ installs in the first week after the relaunch.

Businesses there pay suppliers, staff and partners across many currencies, and cross-border payments are still slow and expensive. Stablecoin payouts and settlement solve exactly that problem. Hong Kong and Singapore now have stablecoin rules, which gives enterprises the confidence to start. I know this region well. I have taken a product to market in Malaysia, and I speak Mandarin, Cantonese and Malay. I would like to help BVNK grow there.

I can share references from product marketing, compliance and legal colleagues at Blockchain.com.
