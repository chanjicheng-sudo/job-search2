# BVNK (Mastercard) — Product Marketing Manager: hiring-manager read

**This is a much bigger reach than Kraken, and the CV was built anyway at her request.**

## Why it is a reach

- **The salary band.** $180,000–$210,000 USD, and the JD says "experienced" and "own and
  scale our sales enablement function". That is a band for someone with six-plus years in
  product marketing who has run this function before. She has roughly twenty months
  post-graduation and is currently an intern. A well-written CV does not close that.
- **It is a different discipline, not a more senior version of hers.** Everything she has
  done is B2C paid acquisition: buying installs from strangers at a unit cost. This role
  is B2B enterprise product marketing, arming a sales team that closes deals with Corpay,
  Worldpay and Deel. The core motion barely overlaps. She has never worked with a sales
  team, never run win/loss analysis, never written positioning or release notes, never
  built enablement.
- **Check the location.** The only posted band is USA. BVNK is London-headquartered so a
  UK req may exist, but if this one is US-based it means sponsorship on top of everything
  else. Confirm before spending effort.

## What she genuinely has

Three things, and two of them were buried on every other CV:

- **Wealth and Institutional at Blockchain.com.** Institutional is B2B. The single most
  relevant line she owns, and it was a trailing clause in every earlier draft.
- **The B2B research work.** The 114-response segmented survey is a voice-of-customer
  programme in miniature. The 100-operator iGaming database and the 80 crypto publisher
  outreach list are B2B segmentation and partner mapping. The closest thing in her
  history to this job.
- **AI automation in marketing workflows.** An explicit requirement, and she is strong on
  it. They name n8n and LLMs; she has the UGC pipeline, the automated Apple Search Ads
  reporting and the Streamlit dashboard.

Plus compliance: they want someone who sets comms standards "working with Legal and
Compliance", and she has done exactly that in two roles.

## Recommendation

Do not expect a result from this req as written. Two better options:

1. **Apply to a different door at the same company.** BVNK says plainly "you may be a
   great fit for this role or another role in our team". Check their board for growth,
   lifecycle or associate product marketing. She is a real candidate for those and a long
   shot for this one.
2. **If she wants this one regardless** — which is what was built — the CV is rebuilt
   around Institutional, customer research and automation, with paid acquisition demoted
   to one line. That is a genuinely different document, not a reshuffle.

## Before sending

- **The Malaysia bullet has no outcome**, because the work is in flight. A partner count
  or a launch date converts it from activity to progress. Right now it is the
  weakest-evidenced strong item on the page.
- **Delete the `[ADD: n8n]` note**, which is a message to her, not to them. n8n is
  learnable in a weekend, and doing that would let her answer the automation requirement
  with something she built rather than something she could build.
- **The 114-response survey attribution** needs confirming. See OPEN-QUESTIONS.md. It is
  the opening line of this CV, so it is worth being certain.
